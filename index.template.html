The Ranger from the Quake series drops into Doom with 20 Quake Champions weapons, his Dire Orb, and Quake movement.

The weapons use actual Quake Champions Blender source files and textures, reanimated in Blender with ambient weapon movements inspired by Quake 2.

Arsenal

Doom weapon spots roll from pools of Quake guns, so each run plays differently. Alt-fire on any gun gives a light zoom. Fuel is a separate ammo pool shared by the Flamethrower, Tormentor and Eelsbane. Three rare monster drops: Mancubi can drop Tormentor, Arachnotrons Eelsbane, and Arch-viles Hellraiser. Ordinary Doom II monsters and compatible replacements count, so no extra mod is needed.

Press J to hold your weapon right, centered or left, as in Quake 2. Each hand position has its own animations, and shots leave from that muzzle. The crosshair stays steady as you look around, with alignment for the selected weapon and hand. Every clip below shows all three.

Dire Orb

Press F to throw the orb, then F again to teleport and telefrag enemies at your destination.

The orb lasts 5 seconds, with a 30-second cooldown. Rare hourglass pickups shorten the cooldown.

Movement

  • Quake 2 ground physics with CPM air control: hold forward in the air to steer your speed.
  • Ground slide from Quake Champions: crouch as you land at speed.
  • Weapon jumps: rocket and grenade jumps, plasma wall-climbs, nail boosts. Your own splash damage is cut to 25%.
  • Boot kick (R): knocks enemies back, opens doors, flips switches, and pushes you off walls.

More Quake in your Doom

  • Armor and powerups

    Green, Yellow and Red armor tiers. Megahealth drains back to 100. Quad Damage, Protection, Bandolier and Adrenaline, with expiry warnings.

  • Gore

    Kills vaporize, burn or get pinned to walls, and corpses gib when you shoot them.

  • Quake 2-style HUD

    A Ranger face and the orb cooldown, plus crosshairs that switch by weapon type. The HUD Position option puts it at the top or bottom of the screen.

  • Presentation

    3D weapon and pickup models, first-person legs, custom shaders, dynamic lights, the Ranger's voice, and title music.

Doomed Ranger will be released on itch.io. You'll need UZDoom (the mod targets ZScript 4.14.0) with OpenGL or Vulkan, and a Doom IWAD. Doom II is recommended so the Super Shotgun spawns.

Rebind keys under Customize Controls → Doomed Ranger. Choose HUD Position: Top / Bottom under Options → Doomed Ranger.

Once released, launch with uzdoom -iwad doom2.wad -file DoomedRanger.pk3.
